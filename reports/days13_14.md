## Rehearsal (`scripts/demo_rehearsal.py`)

The four video turns, run against the live demo:

| | before (build fb98742) | after (build c270b53) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. German search, Porto or Valencia | 6 cards, **all Valencia**; the answer said Porto had none | 6 cards, 3 per city |
| 2. "Is #2 overpriced?" | band + not-a-valuation line | same |
| 3. Schools and transport compared | cited in German; **uncited in 3 of 3 English runs** | cited, Sources list, 2022 page dated |
| 4. Contact for #2 | consented link, nothing sent | same |

Fixes (`c270b53`):

1. **One search per place.** A single multi-place filter returns whichever place
   sorts first. The model filled the gap with "Porto had no results", which wasn't true.
   Each place now gets its own `filter_listings` call (up to 3 places). The cards are
   grouped by place and renumbered, and each place's count goes to the model.
2. **`【S1】` citations.** Nemotron often writes our source numbers in its own
   full-width brackets. The strip meant for its `【{"id":…}】` markup removed them
   along with it, so every English area answer lost its citations. They're now read as
   `[S1]` first. As a backstop, an answer that still cites nothing gets one retry with
   the rule restated. Added to `docs/FEEDBACK.md` #2.
3. **Source dates.** Tavily general results carry no `published_date`, and the model
   dated a 2022 announcement to 2026. We now take the date from the URL when it has one,
   and the skill no longer states dates the source doesn't give.

Tests: 63 pass (multi-place merge, wide-bracket citations, citation retry, URL dates).
