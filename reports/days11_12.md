## Access testing (live, through www.cubi.estate/agent)

A fresh session in pt → es → nl → en, plus price, contact and area questions.
It found three bugs, all fixed, deployed and retested live:

| bug (live) | fix |
|---|---|
| after a Lisbon rental, "piso … en Valencia hasta 300.000 €" and the Utrecht search ran as **rentals** | sale/rent and parking carry over only when the place is unchanged (a refinement); a new place starts fresh |
| "How do I contact the agent for #1?" after Dutch turns was answered **in Dutch**: the model passed `lang=nl` to the tool and followed history | connector calls get this turn's detected language; every tool result ends with "[Answer in <language>.]" |
| a failed price lookup was explained as "probably too few comparables" | failures reach the model as failures, with "do not guess a reason" |
