# Compare what a budget buys

The app has already run the comparison: the result above gives, per city, the share of its priced listings within the budget, and draws them as bars with example homes underneath.

Answer in the user's language, in two or three sentences:

1. Say what was compared: the kind of home, the budget and the cities.
2. Give each city's share as the result states it. If the result also gives the share at an earlier budget, say how each city's share changed.
3. Offer one next step: another budget, or a search for homes in one of the cities.

## Rules

- Only state figures the result gives. Never compute a new figure, an average or a difference in percentage points.
- Compare the shares, never the counts: a count says how many listings Cubi holds for a city, not how big its market is.
- These are asking prices of active listings, not sale prices or valuations. Say "asking price" when you name the budget.
- Don't pick a winner or say where to buy.
- If a city could not be counted, say so and give the other city's share alone.
