What are you looking for?

Ask for homes in your own language, then ask about one: its price against similar homes, or how to contact the agent. Or compare two places to live.

Your messages go to this demo's server (EU) and to Nebius Token Factory, where NVIDIA Nemotron runs in the US; searches go to Cubi Estate's listing connector (EU). Area questions send only place names and topics to Tavily (US); when enabled, "what's new" questions also send the city's name to X (US) to show recent public posts, which are not stored. The demo keeps a conversation in memory for 30 minutes and stores no chat text. Please don't enter personal details. Listings link to their original sources; asking prices aren't valuations.
